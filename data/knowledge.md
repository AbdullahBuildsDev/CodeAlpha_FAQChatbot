# CodeAlpha Knowledge Base

## About CodeAlpha
CodeAlpha is a software development company that provides internships and real-world
projects in domains such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Web Development,
Python Programming, and Data Science. It focuses on giving students practical,
hands-on experience and a verified completion certificate.

## The Internship Program
The internship usually runs for one month. Interns are assigned real tasks in their
chosen domain and work on them at their own pace within the given time frame. The goal
is to build practical skills, create portfolio projects, and gain industry exposure.
No prior professional experience is required — the program is designed for students
and beginners.

## How to Apply
You can apply for a CodeAlpha internship through the official website at
www.codealpha.tech by filling out the application form. After selection, you receive
an offer letter and your assigned tasks.

## Cost
The CodeAlpha internship is free to join. There is no fee for participating or for
receiving the certificate.

## Tasks and Completion
Each intern must complete a minimum of two or three tasks from their domain to be
eligible for the certificate. Submitting only one task is considered incomplete, and
no certificate will be issued in that case. Tasks are designed to reflect real
industry work.

## Submitting Your Work
Upload your project source code to GitHub in a repository named CodeAlpha_ProjectName.
If you are not comfortable with GitHub, you may upload the complete project to Google
Drive instead. Then submit the link through the official submission form shared in your
WhatsApp group. You should also post a video explanation of your project on LinkedIn,
tagging @CodeAlpha and including the GitHub repository link.

## Certificates and Rewards
After successfully completing the required tasks, you receive a Completion Certificate
(QR verified) and a Unique ID Certificate. Based on your performance, you may also
receive a Letter of Recommendation. The program additionally offers placement support
and resume building support.

## Perks
Internship perks include: an Internship Offer Letter, a Completion Certificate,
a Unique ID Certificate, a Letter of Recommendation (performance based), job and
placement support, and resume building support.

## Deadlines
Task submission is open throughout the internship month. The exact last date for
submission is shared in your official WhatsApp group, so always check there for the
current deadline.

## Support and Contact
For any questions about your internship, tasks, or certificates, you can contact
CodeAlpha support by email at services@codealpha.tech, or through the WhatsApp number
and official group provided to you. The website www.codealpha.tech also has details
and updates.
